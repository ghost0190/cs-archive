Scratch

It’s 时间 to choose your own adventure! Your 作业, quite simply, is to implement in Scratch any 项目 of your choice, be it an interactive story, game, animation, or anything else, subject only to the following requirements:

  • Your 项目 must have 在 least two sprites, 在 least one of which must resemble something other than a cat.
  • Your 项目 must have 在 least three scripts total (i.e., not necessarily three per sprite).
  • Your 项目 must use 在 least one condition.
  • Your 项目 must use 在 least one loop.
  • Your 项目 must use 在 least one variable.
  • Your 项目 must use 在 least one sound.
  • Your 项目 should be 更多 complex than most of those demonstrated in 讲座 (many of which, though instructive, were quite short) but it can be 较少 complex than Ivy’s Hardest Game. As such, your 项目 should probably use a few dozen puzzle pieces overall.

If you’d like to try out some Scratch 项目 from past students, here are a few:

  • It’s Raining Men, from 讲座
  • Ivy’s Hardest Game, a game, Harvard edition
  • Soccer, a game
  • Cookie Love Story, an animation
  • Gingerbread tales, an interactive story
  • Intersection, a game
  • Oscartime, a game

You might find these tutorials or starter 项目 helpful. And you’re 欢迎 to explore Scratch.mit.edu for inspiration. But try to think of an idea on your own, and then set out to implement it. However, don’t try to implement the entirety of your 项目 all 在 once: pluck off one piece 在 a 时间. In other words, take baby steps: write a bit of code (i.e., drag and drop a few puzzle pieces), test, write a bit 更多, test, and so forth. And select File > Save now every few minutes so that you don’t lose any work!

If, along the way, you find it too difficult to implement some feature, try not to fret; alter your design or work around the 问题. If you set out to implement an idea that you find fun, odds are you won’t find it too hard to satisfy the above requirements.

Alright, off you go. Make us proud!

Once finished with your 项目, select File > Save now one last 时间. Then select File > Save to your computer and keep that file so that you can 提交 it. If prompted by your computer to 打开 or Save the file, be sure to Save it.

你好, 世界

Suffice it to say it’s a bit harder to meet classmates when taking a 课程 online. But, thanks to technology, everyone can 在 least say 你好!

If you have a 电话 (or digital camera) and would like to say 你好 to classmates, record a 1- to 2-minute 视频 of yourself saying 你好, perhaps stating where in the 世界 you are, why you’re taking CS50x, and something interesting 关于 you! Try to begin your 视频 by saying “你好, 世界” and 结束 it with “my 姓名 is…, and 这是 CS50.” But, ultimately, it’s totally up to you.

If you do record a 视频, upload it to YouTube (unless blocked in your country, in which case you’re 欢迎 to upload it elsewhere) so that you can provide us with its URL when you 提交!
