CS50 AP is an amalgam of two 课程, CS50’s 计算机科学导论, otherwise known as CS50x, and CS50’s Understanding Technology, otherwise known as CS50T. CS50 AP is only for students in high school. Students not in high school should take CS50T and CS50x instead.

CS50 AP is also available via edX.

关于 this 课程

这是 CS50 AP, 哈佛大学’s introduction to the intellectual enterprises of 计算机科学 and the art of 编程 for students in high school, which satisfies the College Board’s AP 计算机科学 Principles (CSP) curriculum framework. Students in high school 五月 receive AP 信用卡 for this 课程 provided their school approves the 信用卡, submits the student’s work on the College Board’s Create Performance Task by 星期二, 四月 30, 2024, 11:59 PM Eastern 时间, and submits the student’s work on the 结束-of-课程 Multiple-Choice Exam by 星期三, 五月 15, 2024, 12:00 PM local 时间. See further details 在 the College Board’s 2023-2024 AP Exam Dates page. Students should make all such arrangements in advance with their school directly. Students who earn a satisfactory score on all 问题集 and a 最终项目 are also eligible to receive a verified 证书 from HarvardX.

An entry-level 课程 for students with no prior 背景, taught by David J. Malan, CS50 AP teaches students how to think algorithmically and solve 问题 efficiently. Topics include abstraction, 算法, 数据结构, encapsulation, 资源 management, 安全, software engineering, and Web 编程. Languages include C, Python, and SQL plus HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. 问题集 inspired by the arts, humanities, social sciences, and sciences. 课程 culminates in a 最终项目.
