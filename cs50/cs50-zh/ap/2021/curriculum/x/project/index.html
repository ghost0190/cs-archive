最终项目

The climax of this 课程 is its 最终项目. The 最终项目 is your opportunity to take your newfound savvy with 编程 out for a spin and develop your very own piece of software. So long as your 项目 draws upon this 课程’s lessons, the nature of your 项目 is entirely up to you. You 五月 implement your 项目 in any language(s). You are 欢迎 to utilize infrastructure other than the CS50 IDE. All that we ask is that you build something of interest to you, that you solve an actual problem, that you impact your 社区, or that you change the 世界. Strive to create something that outlives this 课程.

Inasmuch as software development is rarely a one-person effort, you are allowed an opportunity to collaborate with one or two classmates for this 最终项目. Needless to say, it is expected that every student in any such group contribute equally to the design and implementation of that group’s 项目. Moreover, it is expected that the scope of a two- or three-person group’s 项目 be, respectively, twice or thrice that of a typical one-person 项目. A one-person 项目, mind you, should entail 更多 时间 and effort than is 必需 by each of the 课程’s 问题集.

Ideas

  • a web-based application using JavaScript, Python, and SQL, based in part on the web track’s distribution code
  • an iOS app using Swift
  • a game using Lua with LÖVE
  • an Android app using Java
  • a Chrome extension using JavaScript
  • a command-line program using C
  • a hardware-based application for which you program some device
  • …

如何提交

Step 1 of 2

Create a README.md text file that explains your 项目 and save it in a new folder called 项目 in your ~/ directory. 笔记 that your 项目 源代码 itself does not need to be submitted, but this README.md file must.

Execute the below from within your ~/project directory, logging in with your GitHub username and password when prompted. For 安全, you’ll see asterisks instead of the actual characters in your password.

submit50 cs50/problems/2020/x/project

Step 2 of 2

提交 a short 视频 (that’s no 更多 than 2 minutes in length) in which you present your 项目 to the 世界, as with 幻灯片, screenshots, voiceover, and/or live action. Your 视频 should somehow include your 项目’s title, your 姓名, your city and country, and any other details that you’d like to convey to viewers. See https://www.howtogeek.com/205742/how-to-record-your-windows-mac-linux-android-or-ios-screen/ for tips on how to make a “screencast,” though you’re 欢迎 to use an actual camera. Upload your 视频 to YouTube (or, if blocked in your country, a similar site) and take 笔记 of its URL; it’s fine to flag it as “unlisted,” but don’t flag it as “private.”

When ready to 提交 your 视频, 提交 this form!

That’s it! Your 项目 should be graded within a few minutes. If you don’t see any results in your 成绩册, best to resubmit (running the above submit50 command) with only your README.md file this 时间. No need to resubmit your form.
