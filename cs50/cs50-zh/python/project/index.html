最终项目

Once you have solved each of the 课程’s 问题集, it’s 时间 to implement your 最终项目, a Python program of your very own! The design and implementation of your 项目 is entirely up to you, albeit subject to these requirements:

  • Your 项目 must be implemented in Python.
  • Your 项目 must have a main function and three or 更多 additional functions. At least three of those additional functions must be accompanied by tests that can be executed with pytest.
    • Your main function must be in a file called project.py, which should be in the “root” (i.e., top-level folder) of your 项目.
    • Your 3 必需 custom functions other than main must also be in project.py and defined at the same indentation level as main (i.e., not nested under any classes or functions).
    • Your test functions must be in a file called test_project.py, which should also be in the “root” of your 项目. Be sure they have the same 姓名 as your custom functions, prepended with test_ (test_custom_function, for 示例, where custom_function is a function you’ve implemented in project.py).
    • You are 欢迎 to implement additional classes and functions as you see fit beyond the minimum requirement.
  • Implementing your 项目 should entail 更多 时间 and effort than is 必需 by each of the 课程’s 问题集.

  • Any pip-installable libraries that your 项目 requires must be listed, one per line, in a file called requirements.txt in the root of your 项目. This file allows others to install your 项目’s dependencies with pip install -r requirements.txt. See pip’s documentation for 更多 details. For 示例, if your 项目 uses requests and cowsay, your requirements.txt might look like:

      cowsay
  requests

入门

Creating an entire 项目 五月 seem daunting. Here are some 问题 that you should think 关于 as you start:

  • What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?
  • What new skills will you need to acquire? What topics will you need to research?
  • If working with one or two classmates, who will do what?
  • In the 世界 of software, most everything takes longer to implement than you expect. And so it’s not uncommon to accomplish 较少 in a fixed amount of 时间 than you hope. What might you consider to be a good outcome for your 项目? A better outcome? The best outcome?

Consider making goal milestones to keep you on track.

如何提交

You must complete all three steps!

Step 1 of 3

Create a short 视频 (that’s no 更多 than 3 minutes in length) in which you present your 项目 to the 世界. Your 视频 must begin with an opening 小组课 that displays:

  • your 项目’s title;
  • your 姓名;
  • your GitHub and edX usernames;
  • your city and country;
  • and, the 日期 you have recorded this 视频.

It should then go on to demonstrate your 项目 in action, as with 幻灯片, screenshots, voiceover, and/or live action. See howtogeek.com/205742/how-to-record-your-windows-mac-linux-android-or-ios-screen for tips on how to make a “screencast,” though you’re 欢迎 to use an actual camera. Upload your 视频 to YouTube (or, if blocked in your country, a similar site) and take 笔记 of its URL; it’s fine to flag it as “unlisted,” but don’t flag it as “private.”

提交 this form!

Step 2 of 3

Create a README.md text file (named exactly that!) in your ~/project folder that explains your 项目. This file should include your 项目 title, the URL of your 视频 (created in step 1 above) and a description of your 项目. You 五月 use the below as a template.

    # YOUR PROJECT TITLE
    #### Video Demo:  <URL HERE>
    #### Description:
    TODO

If unfamiliar with Markdown syntax, you might find GitHub’s Basic Writing and Formatting Syntax helpful. If you are using the CS50 Codespace and are prompted to “打开 in CS50 Lab”, you can simply press cancel to 打开 in the Editor. You can also preview your .md file by clicking the ‘preview’ icon as explained here: Markdown Preview in vscode. Standard software 项目 READMEs can often run into the thousands or tens of thousands of words in length; yours need not be that long, but should at least be several hundred words that describe things in detail!

Your README.md file should be minimally multiple paragraphs in length, and should explain what your 项目 is, what each of the files you wrote for the 项目 contains and does, and if you debated certain design choices, explaining why you made them. Ensure you allocate sufficient 时间 and energy to writing a README.md that documents your 项目 thoroughly. Be proud of it! A README.md in the neighborhood of 500 words is likely to be sufficient for describing your 项目 and all aspects of its functionality. If unable to reach that threshold, that probably means your 项目 is insufficiently complex.

Execute the submit50 command below from within your ~/project directory (or from whichever directory contains README.md file and your 项目’s code, which must also be submitted). If your 项目 does not meet all the requirements above, it 五月 be rejected, so be sure you have satisfied all of the bullet points atop this 规格说明 and written a thorough README:

submit50 cs50/problems/2022/python/project
Trouble Submitting?

If you encounter issues because your 项目 is too large, try to ZIP all of the 目录 of that directory (except for README.md) and then 提交 that instead. If still too large, try removing certain configuration files, reducing the size of your submission below 100MB, or try to upload directly using GitHub’s web interface by visiting github.com/me50/USERNAME (where USERNAME is your own GitHub username) and manually dragging and dropping folders, ensuring that when uploading you are doing so to your cs50/problems/2022/python/project branch, otherwise the system will not be able to check it!

Step 3 of 3

That’s it! Your 项目 should be graded within a few minutes. Be sure to visit your 成绩册 at cs50.me/cs50p a few minutes after you 提交.
