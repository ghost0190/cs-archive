小组课

讲座 are supplemented by weekly, 2-hour 45-minute 小组课 led by the 课程’s preceptors and teaching fellows. Attendance at 小组课 is expected.

Schedule

You need only attend (and register via my.harvard.edu for) one of the below:

  • Tuesdays, 9am-11:45am in Pierce 301 with Yuliia Zhukovets, Preceptor, and Nadine Han, Teaching Fellow.
  • Tuesdays, 3:45pm-6:30pm in SEC LL2.229 with Inno Munai and Alex Oort Alonso, Teaching Fellows.
  • Wednesdays, 9am-11:45am in Maxwell Dworkin G125 with Carter Zenke, Preceptor, and Sedik Sadik, Teaching Fellow.
  • Wednesdays, 6pm-8:45pm in Pierce 301 with Kamryn Ohly and Varsha Venkatasubramanian, Teaching Fellows.

Attendance at one of the four weekly 小组课 is expected each 周 unless simultaneously enrolled in another class with the permission of heads@cs50.harvard.edu.
