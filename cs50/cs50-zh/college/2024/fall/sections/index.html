小组课

讲座 are supplemented by weekly 小组课 led by the 课程’s teaching fellows. Different 小组课 are offered for those 较少 comfortable, 更多 comfortable, and somewhere in between.

Attendance at 小组课 is expected.

Schedule

The 课程’s 小组课 schedule is available at cs50.ly/小组课.

  • If you have a one-时间 conflict with your assigned 小组课, you 五月 attend any other 小组课 for the 周 in which you have a conflict: just 电子邮件 heads@cs50.harvard.edu, cc’ing your Teaching Fellow and the Teaching Fellow whose 小组课 you plan to attend.
  • If you have a recurring academic or athletic conflict with your assigned 小组课, 提交 the 小组课 re-作业 form at cs50.ly/resection.
