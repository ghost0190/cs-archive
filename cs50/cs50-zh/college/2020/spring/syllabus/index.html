Learning Objectives

Among the overarching goals for students individually in this 课程 is that they learn something that we haven’t taught them, as is manifest at term’s end by so many students’ final 项目 that use languages, libraries, 工具, and techniques not taught in the 课程. Along the way will students learn to

Ultimately, the 课程 provides students with a foundation for further studies in 计算机科学 and empowers students to apply 计算机科学 to problems in other domains.

The 课程’s philosophy on 学术诚信 is best stated as “be reasonable.” The 课程 recognizes that interactions with classmates and others can facilitate mastery of the 课程’s material. Collaboration on 问题集 is not permitted except to the extent that you 五月 ask classmates and others for help so long as that help does not reduce to another doing your work for you. Collaboration on the 课程’s quizzes and test is not permitted at all. Collaboration on the 课程’s 最终项目 is permitted to the extent prescribed by its 规格说明.

If you commit some act that is not reasonable but bring it to the attention of the 课程’s heads within 72 hours, the 课程 五月 impose local sanctions that 五月 include an unsatisfactory or failing grade for work submitted, but the 课程 will not refer the matter for further disciplinary action except in cases of repeated acts.

If in doubt as to whether some act is reasonable, do not commit it until you solicit and receive approval in writing from the 课程’s heads. Acts considered not reasonable by the 课程 are handled harshly.
