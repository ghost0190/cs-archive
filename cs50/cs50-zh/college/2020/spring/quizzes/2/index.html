Quiz 2

Quizzes are short 作业 due after each 讲座 that allow you to apply each 周’s concepts to new problems. Each quiz is 打开-book: you 五月 use any and all non-human 资源 during a quiz, but the only humans to whom you 五月 turn for help or from whom you 五月 receive help are the 课程’s heads.

What to Do

  1. 打开 this quiz in Google Docs.
  2. Make a copy of the quiz in your own Google account by choosing File → Make a Copy.
  3. 答案 each of the 问题 in the quiz document by filling in the blanks marked TODO.

When to Do It

By 3:00pm on Tue 2/11.

如何提交

  1. 下载 your completed quiz as a PDF by choosing File → 下载 → PDF Document, and save it to your computer.
  2. Go to CS50’s Gradescope page.
  3. Click Quiz 2.
  4. Click 提交 PDF.
  5. Click Select PDF and choose your quiz file.
  6. Click Upload PDF.
  7. Click 问题 1 in the 问题 Outline at left, then click the page (or pages) on which your response to 问题 1 is located. Repeat this process for 问题 2 and 3.
  8. Click 提交.

You should see a message that says “Quiz 2 submitted successfully!”

If you run into any trouble with the above steps, 电子邮件 heads@cs50.harvard.edu!
