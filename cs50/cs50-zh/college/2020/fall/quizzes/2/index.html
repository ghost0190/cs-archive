如何提交

  1. 下载 your completed quiz as a PDF by choosing File → 下载 → PDF Document, and save it to your computer.
  2. Go to CS50’s Gradescope page.
  3. Click Quiz 2.
  4. Click 提交 PDF.
  5. Click Select PDF and choose your quiz file.
  6. Click Upload PDF.
  7. Click 问题 1 in the 问题 Outline at left, then click the page (or pages) on which your response to 问题 1 is located. Repeat this process for 问题 2 and 3.
  8. Click 提交.

You should see a message that says “Quiz 2 submitted successfully!”
