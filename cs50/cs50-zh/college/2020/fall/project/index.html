The climax of this 课程 is its 最终项目. The 最终项目 is your opportunity to take your newfound savvy with 编程 out for a spin and develop your very own piece of software. So long as your 项目 draws upon the 课程’s lessons, the nature of your 项目 is entirely up to you, albeit subject to the 工作人员’s approval. You 五月 implement your 项目 in any language(s) as long as the 工作人员 approves. You are 欢迎 to utilize any infrastructure, provided the 工作人员 ultimately has access to any hardware and software that your 项目 requires. All that we ask is that you build something of interest to you, that you solve an actual problem, that you impact campus, or that you change the 世界. Strive to create something that outlives this 课程.

Inasmuch as software development is rarely a one-person effort, you are allowed an opportunity to collaborate with one or two classmates for this 最终项目. Needless to say, it is expected that every student in any such group contribute equally to the design and implementation of that group’s 项目. Moreover, it is expected that the scope of a two- or three-person group’s 项目 be, respectively, twice or thrice that of a typical one-person 项目. A one-person 项目, mind you, should entail 更多 时间 and effort than is 必需 by each of the 课程’s 问题集. Although no 更多 than three students 五月 design and implement a given 项目, you are 欢迎 to solicit advice from others, so long as you respect the 课程’s policy on 学术诚信.

Extensions on the 最终项目 are not ordinarily granted, except in cases of emergency.

Combining 课程

If taking some other 课程 this semester that has a 最终项目, you are 欢迎 and encouraged to combine this 课程’s 项目 and that 课程’s 项目 into one, toward an end of applying lessons learned in CS50 to some other field, so long as the joint 项目 satisfies this 课程’s and that 课程’s expectations. Before pursuing a joint 项目, though, you must disclose to both 课程 and receive approval from both 课程.

Proposal

due by

The proposal is your opportunity to receive approval and counsel from the 工作人员 before you proceed to design. If collaborating with one or two classmates, each of you should 提交 a proposal, even if identical.

The 工作人员 will either approve your proposal or require modifications on your part for subsequent approval. Your proposal, even if approved, is not binding; you 五月 alter your plan at any point, provided you obtain the 工作人员’s approval for any modifications. 项目 submitted without approval 五月 not receive credit.

Here’s how to complete your proposal.

Log into CS50 IDE and then, in a terminal window:

  1. Execute cd to ensure that you’re in ~/ (i.e., your 首页 directory).
  2. If you haven’t already, execute mkdir project to make (i.e., create) a directory called project in your 首页 directory.
  3. Execute cd project to change into (i.e., 打开) that directory.
  4. Execute wget https://cdn.cs50.net/2020/fall/project/proposal.zip to 下载 a (compressed) ZIP file.
  5. Execute unzip proposal.zip to uncompress that file.
  6. Execute rm proposal.zip followed by yes or y to delete that ZIP file.
  7. Execute ls. You should see a directory called proposal, which was inside of that ZIP file.
  8. Execute cd proposal to change into that directory.
  9. Execute ls. You should see a file called README.md therein.

Edit that file in CS50 IDE, answering the 问题 therein. To 提交 your proposal, follow these instructions.

  1. 下载 your proposal’s README.md file by control-clicking on the file in CS50 IDE’s file browser and choosing 下载.
  2. Go to CS50’s Gradescope page.
  3. Click “最终项目: Proposal”.
  4. Drag and drop your README.md file to the area that says “Drag & Drop”.
  5. Click “Upload”.

You should see a message that says “最终项目: Proposal submitted successfully!”

Implementation

due by

Ultimately due are implementation and documentation of your 最终项目. Your submission thereof must include all of the below.

  1. Documentation for your 项目 in the form of a Markdown file called README.md. This documentation is to be a user’s manual for your 项目. Though the structure of your documentation is entirely up to you, it should be incredibly clear to the 工作人员 how and where, if applicable, to compile, configure, and use your 项目. Your documentation should be at least several paragraphs in length. It should not be necessary for us to contact you with 问题 regarding your 项目 after its submission. Hold our hand with this documentation; be sure to 答案 in your documentation any 问题 that you think we might have while 测试 your work.
  2. A “design document” for your 项目 in the form of a Markdown file called DESIGN.md that discusses, technically, how you implemented your 项目 and why you made the design decisions you did. Your design document should be at least several paragraphs in length. Whereas your documentation is meant to be a user’s manual, consider your design document your opportunity to give the 工作人员 a technical tour of your 项目 underneath its hood.
  3. Any and all files 必需 to run your software (even if intended for some infrastructure other than CS50 IDE), including 源代码 as well as, if applicable, configuration files, Makefiles, sample inputs, SQLite 数据库, and so forth. Needless to say, all 源代码 should be thoroughly commented.
  4. A short 视频 (that’s no 更多 than 2 minutes in length) in which you present your 项目 to the 世界, as with 幻灯片, screenshots, voiceover, and/or live action. Your 视频 should somehow include your 项目’s title, your 姓名 and year, your dorm/house and concentration, and any other details that you’d like to convey to viewers. See CS171’s tips on how to make a “screencast” though you’re 欢迎 to use an actual camera. Upload your 视频 to YouTube as “public” or “unlisted” and include the URL of the 视频 in your README.md file.
