This test is 打开-book: you 五月 use any and all non-human 资源 during the test, but the only humans to whom you 五月 turn for help or from whom you 五月 receive help are the 课程’s heads, which means that

you 五月

  • browse and 搜索 the 互联网,
  • review books,
  • review 问题 and 答案 already posted on Ed,
  • review the 课程’s own materials,
  • use CS50 IDE,
  • 电子邮件 the 课程’s heads at heads@cs50.harvard.edu with 问题, but

you 五月 not

  • receive or solicit directly or indirectly any help from anyone other than the 课程’s heads.

Take care to review the 课程’s policy on 学术诚信 in its entirety. 笔记 particularly, but not only, that

  • looking at another individual’s work during the test is not reasonable and
  • turning to humans (besides the 课程’s heads) for help or receiving help from humans (besides the 课程’s heads) during the test is not reasonable.

Unless otherwise noted, you 五月 call any functions we’ve encountered this term in code that you write. You needn’t comment code that you write, but comments 五月 help in cases of partial credit. If having difficulty with code, you 五月 resort to pseudocode for potential partial credit.

Among the test’s aims is to assess your newfound comfort with the 课程’s material and your ability to apply the 课程’s lessons to familiar and unfamiliar problems. And most problems aspire to teach something new.

You 五月 resubmit as many times as you would like before the test’s 截止时间. Late submissions incur penalties per the 课程’s 教学大纲.

     unzip test.zip

如何提交

  1. 下载 a ZIP file of your test folder (which should contain folders for finsta, flaskless, and shark) by control-clicking on your test folder in CS50 IDE’s file browser and choosing 下载.
  2. Go to CS50’s Gradescope page.
  3. Click Test: Code Files.
  4. Drag and drop your test.zip file to the area that says “Drag & Drop”.
  5. Click “Upload”.
  6. You should see a message that says “Test: Code Files submitted successfully!”
  7. 下载 your completed test response document from Google Docs as a PDF by choosing File → 下载 → PDF Document, and save it to your computer.
  8. Go to CS50’s Gradescope page.
  9. Click Test: Response Document.
  10. Click 提交 PDF.
  11. Click Select PDF and choose your test file.
  12. Click Upload PDF.
  13. For each 问题 in the 问题 Outline at left, click on the 问题 and then click on the page (or pages) on which your response to that 问题 is located.
  14. Click 提交.
  15. You should see a message that says “Test: Response Document submitted successfully!”

If you run into any trouble with the above steps, 电子邮件 heads@cs50.harvard.edu!
