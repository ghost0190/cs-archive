The climax of this 课程 is its 最终项目. The 最终项目 is your opportunity to take your newfound savvy with 编程 out for a spin and develop your very own piece of software. So long as your 项目 draws upon the 课程’s lessons, the nature of your 项目 is entirely up to you, albeit subject to the 工作人员’s approval. You 五月 implement your 项目 in any language(s) as long as the 工作人员 approves. You are 欢迎 to utilize any infrastructure, provided the 工作人员 ultimately has access to any hardware and software that your 项目 requires. All that we ask is that you build something of interest to you, that you solve an actual problem, that you impact campus, or that you change the 世界. Strive to create something that outlives this 课程.

Inasmuch as software development is rarely a one-person effort, you are allowed an opportunity to collaborate with one or two classmates for this 最终项目. Needless to say, it is expected that every student in any such group contribute equally to the design and implementation of that group’s 项目. Moreover, it is expected that the scope of a two- or three-person group’s 项目 be, respectively, twice or thrice that of a typical one-person 项目. A one-person 项目, mind you, should entail 更多 时间 and effort than is 必需 by each of the 课程’s 问题集. Although no 更多 than three students 五月 design and implement a given 项目, you are 欢迎 to solicit advice from others, so long as you respect the 课程’s policy on 学术诚信.

Extensions on the 最终项目 will not be granted.

Ideas

Here are just some of the possibilities. Discuss any and all with the the 工作人员! And if you’d like to solicit collaborators for an idea you have, do post in Ed!

  • an iOS app using Swift
  • an Android app using Java

  • a command-line program using C
  • a hardware-based application for which you program some device
  • a web-based application using Node.js
  • a game using Lua with LÖVE
  • …

Combining 课程

If taking some other 课程 this semester that has a 最终项目, you are 欢迎 and encouraged to combine this 课程’s 项目 and that 课程’s 项目 into one, toward an end of applying lessons learned in CS50 to some other field, so long as the joint 项目 satisfies this 课程’s and that 课程’s expectations. Before pursuing a joint 项目, though, you must disclose to both 课程 and receive approval from both 课程.

Extensions on the 最终项目 are not granted, except in cases of emergency.

The 工作人员 will either approve your proposal or require modifications on your part for subsequent approval. Your proposal, even if approved, is not binding; you 五月 alter your plan at any point, provided you obtain the 工作人员’s approval for any modifications. 项目 submitted without approval 五月 not receive credit.

Implementation

Gradescope will not allow you to upload your final implementation until after the Status Report 截止时间 has passed.

due by 2026-05-10T23:59:00-04:00

Ultimately due are implementation and documentation of your 最终项目. Your submission thereof must include all of the below.

  1. Documentation for your 项目 in the form of a Markdown file called README.md. This documentation is to be a user’s manual for your 项目. Though the structure of your documentation is entirely up to you, it should be incredibly clear to the 工作人员 how and where, if applicable, to compile, configure, and use your 项目. Your documentation should be at least several paragraphs in length. It should not be necessary for us to contact you with 问题 regarding your 项目 after its submission. Hold our hand with this documentation; be sure to 答案 in your documentation any 问题 that you think we might have while 测试 your work.
  2. A “design document” for your 项目 in the form of a Markdown file called DESIGN.md that discusses, technically, how you implemented your 项目 and why you made the design decisions you did. Your design document should be at least several paragraphs in length. Whereas your documentation is meant to be a user’s manual, consider your design document your opportunity to give the 工作人员 a technical tour of your 项目 underneath its hood.
  3. Any and all files 必需 to run your software (even if intended for some infrastructure other than cs50.dev), including 源代码 as well as, if applicable, configuration files, Makefiles, sample inputs, SQLite 数据库, and so forth. Needless to say, all 源代码 should be thoroughly commented.
  4. A short 视频 (that’s no 更多 than 3 minutes in length) in which you present your 项目 to the 世界, as with 幻灯片, screenshots, voiceover, and/or live action, ideally with you yourself appearing in the 视频. Your 视频 should somehow include your 项目’s title, your 姓名 and year, your dorm/house and concentration, and any other details that you’d like to convey to viewers. See CS1710’s tips on how to make a “screencast” though you’re 欢迎 to use a phone or actual camera. Upload your 视频 to YouTube as “public” or “unlisted” and include the URL of the 视频 in your README.md file.

For your 最终项目 (and your 最终项目 only!) it is reasonable to use AI-based software other than CS50’s own (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, et al.), but the essence of the work must still be your own. You’ve learned enough to use such 工具 as helpers. Treat such 工具 as amplifying, not supplanting, your productivity.

If your 项目 requires (for execution and 测试) hardware or software other than that offered by VS Code, be sure that the the 工作人员 are aware of and have approved your 项目’s needs.

Step 1 of 2

We recommend you upload your 项目 at least once, prior to 11:00 PM ET. Even if you make a subsequent upload later, at least you have a submission on file if something goes wrong with the upload at the last minute.

  1. 下载 a ZIP file of your 最终项目 by running zip -r project.zip * within your 项目’s folder in VS Code. Find the project.zip file in the file browser and right-click to choose 下载. (If you created your 最终项目 outside of VS Code, you should still create a ZIP file of the 项目 folder.)
  2. Go to CS50’s Gradescope page.
  3. Select 最终项目: Implementation.
  4. Drag and drop your ZIP file to the area that says “Drag & Drop”.
  5. Click “Upload”.

Trouble uploading to Gradescope? (e.g. due to large file count/large file size) Gradescope does have a soft file size limit when uploading (if it times out, Gradescope will reject it). We recommend your ZIP be no larger than 15MB if at all possible; you can probably help trend it in this direction by, if needed, omitting any large assets like images and any datasets that your 项目 requires. At an absolute minimum, and only if none of the above options work, you 五月 upload your README.md and DESIGN.md file to Gradescope, so long as your README.md includes a link to your code files publicly accessible on, e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox, GitHub (not to be edited after the 截止时间!), etc.

You should see a message that says “最终项目: Implementation submitted successfully!”
