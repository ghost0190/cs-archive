CS50 Puzzle Day

2026-09-05T12:00:00-04:00/2026-09-05T16:00:00-04:00
Science & Engineering Complex
150 Western Ave, Allston MA 02134

Click to Register

4 hours of pizza, 谜题 and prizes! Plus a free CS50 Puzzle Day t-shirt! 👕

CS50 Puzzle Day is an opportunity to collaborate on a team with classmates and friends on a packet of 谜题 (i.e., logic problems). Among the event’s goals is to send a message that 计算机科学 is 更多 关于 problem-solving than it is 关于, say, 编程. Among the event’s other goals are prizes.

Teams of size 2, 3, and 4 allowed. 打开 to all Harvard (and MIT) affiliates, whether taking CS50 or not.

谜题 are not 编程 challenges. No prior CS or 编程 experience needed, just logic and smarts! See some past 谜题.
