问题集

问题集 are 编程 作业 that allow you to implement each 周’s concepts in code.

Each 问题集 is also accompanied by a form, in which we will ask several 问题 that ask you to practice applying each 讲座’s concepts. Some of these will be worth points, and count towards your score on this 问题集, and some will not. Among the goals of these 问题 are to have you synthesize knowledge from class alone.

To 答案 these 问题, whether worth points or not, you 五月 use any materials on CS50’s 课程 website (i.e., cs50.harvard.edu), but nothing else. You 五月 not use any other 工具 like cs50.ai or cs50.dev. And you 五月 not 搜索 for information on the 互联网 at large). You 五月 only reference external sources if the 问题 explicitly references that source as well. The only humans to whom you 五月 turn for help or from whom you 五月 receive help on these forms are the 课程’s heads, by posting to Ed under the Form category.
