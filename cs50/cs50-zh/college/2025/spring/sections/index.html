小组课

讲座 are supplemented by weekly, 2-hour 45-minute 小组课 led by the 课程’s preceptors and teaching fellows. Attendance at 小组课 is expected.

If you have a conflict with your assigned 小组课, please 电子邮件 heads@cs50.harvard.edu ahed of your 小组课 时间. Unless it is a sickness related conflict, you’d be expected to attend another 小组课 that 周 to make-up your attendance.

Schedule

You need only attend (and register via my.harvard.edu for) one of the below:

  • Tuesdays, 9am-11:45am in Maxwell Dworkin G125 with Thomas Cardenas and Kelly Ding, Teaching Fellows.
  • Tuesdays, 3:45pm-6:30pm in SEC LL2.229 with Claire Xu and Dhruv Patel, Teaching Fellows.
  • Wednesdays, 9am-11:45am in Maxwell Dworkin G125 with Yuliia Zhukovets, Preceptor, and Natalia Siwek, Teaching Fellow.
  • Wednesdays, 6pm-8:45pm in Maxwell Dworkin G125 with Omer Mujawar and Mohamed Salam Moumie Ntieche, Teaching Fellows.

Attendance at one of the four weekly 小组课 is expected each 周 unless simultaneously enrolled in another class with the permission of heads@cs50.harvard.edu.
