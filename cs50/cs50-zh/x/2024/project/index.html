最终项目

这门课程的高潮是最终项目。最终项目是你将新学到的编程技能付诸实践的机会， spin and develop your very own piece of software. So long as your项目 draws upon 这门课程’s lessons, the nature of your项目 is entirely up to you. You 五月 implement your项目 in any language(s). You are 欢迎 to utilize infrastructure other than the CS50 Codespace. All that we ask is that you build something of interest to you, that you solve an actual problem, that you impact your 社区, or that you change 世界各地. Strive to create something that outlives 这门课程.

由于软件开发很少是一个人的工作，你被允许有机会与 与一两位同学合作 完成此最终项目。不用说，期望组内每个学生做出平等的贡献lly to the design and implementation of that group’s项目。 Moreover, it is expected that the scope of a two- or three-person group’s项目 be, respectively, twice or thrice that of a typical one-person项目。 一份 one-person项目， mind you, should entail 更多时间 and effort than is 必需 by each of 这门课程’s 问题集.

笔记 that CS50’s 工作人员 audits submissions to CS50x including this 最终项目. Students found to be in violation of 学术诚信政策 的学生将被从课程中移除，并被视为不符合证书资格。已完成 CS50x 的学生，如果被发现在最终项目中违规d to be in violation, will have their CS50证书 (and edX证书， if applicable) revoked.

入门

创建一个完整的项目 可能看起来令人生畏. 以下是一些问题 that 你应该思考 在你开始时:

  • 你的软件将做什么? 它将有哪些功能? 它将如何运行?
  • 你需要获取哪些新技能？你需要研究哪些主题？
  • 如果与一两位同学合作，谁将负责什么？
  • In 世界各地 of software, most everything takes longer to implement than you expect. And so it’s not uncommon to accomplish 较少 in a fixed amount of时间 than you hope. What might you consider to be a good outcome for your 项目? 一份 better outcome? The best outcome?

Consider making goal milestones to keep you on track.

If using the CS50 Codespace, 创建一个 directory called project to store your项目 源代码 and other files. You are 欢迎 to develop your项目 outside of the CS50 Codespace.

For 你的最终项目 (and 你的最终项目 only!) it is reasonable to use AI-based software other than CS50’s own (e.g., ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Bing Chat, et al.), but the essence of the work must still be your own. You’ve learned enough to use such 工具 as helpers. Treat such 工具 as amplifying, not supplanting, your productivity. But you still must cite any use of such 工具 in the comments of your code.

如何提交

你必须 complete all three steps!

Step 1 of 3

Create a short视频 (that’s no 更多 than 3 minutes in length) in which you present your项目 to 世界各地. Your视频 must begin with an opening 小组课 that displays:

  • your 项目’s title;
  • your 姓名;
  • your GitHub and edX usernames;
  • your city and country;
  • and, the 日期 you have recorded this视频。

It should then go on to demonstrate your项目 in action, as with 幻灯片, screenshots, voiceover, and/or live action. See howtogeek.com/205742/how-to-record-your-windows-mac-linux-android-or-ios-screen for tips on how to make a “screencast,” though you’re 欢迎 to use an actual camera. Upload your视频 to YouTube (or, if blocked in your country, a similar site) and take 笔记 of its URL; it’s fine to flag it as “unlisted,” but don’t flag it as “private.”

提交 this form!

Step 2 of 3

Create a README.md text file (named exactly that!) in your project folder that explains your项目。 This file should include your项目 Title, the URL of your视频 (created in step 1 above) and a description of your项目。 You 五月 use the below as a template.

# YOUR PROJECT TITLE
#### Video 演示:  <URL HERE>
#### 课程描述:
TODO

If unfamiliar with Markdown syntax, you might find GitHub’s Basic Writing and Formatting Syntax helpful. You can also preview your .md file by clicking the ‘preview’ icon as explained here: Markdown Preview in vscode. Standard software项目 READMEs can often run into the thousands or tens of thousands of words in length; yours need not be that long, but should at least be several hundred words that describe things in detail!

Your README.md file should be minimally multiple paragraphs in length, and should explain what your项目 is, what each of the files you wrote 的项目 contains and does, and if you debated certain design choices, explaining why you made them. Ensure you allocate sufficient时间 and energy to writing a README.md that documents your项目 thoroughly. Be proud of it! 一份 README.md in the neighborhood of 750 words is likely to be sufficient for describing your项目 and all aspects of its functionality. If unable to reach that threshold, that probably 意思s your项目 is insufficiently complex.

Execute the submit50 command below from within your project directory (or from whichever directory contains README.md file and your 项目’s code, which must also be submitted), logging in with your GitHub username and password when prompted. For 安全, you’ll see asterisks instead of the actual characters in your password.

submit50 cs50/problems/2024/x/project
Trouble Submitting?

If you encounter issues because your项目 is too large, try to ZIP all of the 目录 of that directory (except for README.md) and then 提交 that instead. If still too large, try removing certain configuration files, reducing the size of your submission below 100MB, or try to upload directly using GitHub’s web interface by visiting github.com/me50/USERNAME (where USERNAME is your own GitHub username) and manually dragging and dropping folders, ensuring that when uploading you are 这样做 to your cs50/problems/2024/x/project branch, otherwise the system will not be able to check it!

请务必 visit 你的成绩册 at cs50.me/cs50x a few minutes after you 提交. It’s only by loading 你的成绩册 that the system can check to see whether 你已完成 这门课程, and that is also what triggers the (instant) generation of your free CS50证书 and the (within 30 days) generation of the Verified证书 from edX, if you’ve completed all of the other作业。 请务必 claim your free证书 (按照 link at the top of 你的成绩册) before 1 一月 2025.

Don’t skip the above step! 这门课程 is not considered complete until you do the above and see the green banner saying you’ve completed 这门课程. If you do not do the above prior to 1 一月 2025, your status 在该课程中 will be subject to the carryover rules in the 常见问题.
