Recap

  • Over these past ten 周次, you have been drinking from the proverbial firehose.
  • During 这门课程, you learned to program in many different languages; indeed, our great hope is that you learned how to program in them all, regardless of the language.
  • Consider 返回 to the beginning of 这门课程 when you worked on Mario: You have come so far and have gained so many skills.
  • 你应该 measure your success as the delta between your skill at the start of 这门课程 and your skill now, at the end of 这门课程.
  • Further, we hope that you learned how to solve problems above all else, how to take input, process it through an algorithm, and produce some output. To that point, we played a game where we drew a picture using verbal instructions.
  • Notice how some verbal instructions were higher level and others were lower level, like the different 编程 languages utilized in 这门课程.
  • Notice how some instructions were 更多 specific than others. Remember times when you had to refine your code to do exactly as you wanted.
  • Also, notice how some instructions were abstracted away, like draw arms. This is similar to how we abstracted away code into functions.
  • Looking at all the周of 这门课程, you gathered 工具 and built skills. Above all, though, you learned how to teach自己.
  • Your 最终项目 is your opportunity to build a项目， using these skills, that is of your 规格说明 and design. We hope you will join us 的 CS50 Hackathon, an epic all-nighter wherein we work together on our final项目。

Looking Ahead

  • As you journey from the work of 这门课程 to 世界各地 outside CS50, you可能想要 to take a number of steps to prepare and adventure out.
  • Journeying on, 下载 and learn 关于 VS Code.
  • To be able to execute commands on the terminal, much like you did on CS50.dev, install command-line 工具 on your Mac or PC.
  • Learn 更多 关于 Git.
  • Host a website using GitHub or Netlify.
  • If applicable, host a web app using AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • Ask问题 in relevant online 社区.
  • Ask问题 using AI-based 工具 like ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot.
  • Take any of our other CS50 课程.
  • Join one of our many 社区.

  • Many people made 这门课程 possible. Thank you for making 这门课程 possible and for supporting us so well!

总结
