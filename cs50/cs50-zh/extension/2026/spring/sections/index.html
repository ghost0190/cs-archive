Norms

All 小组课 are held online via Zoom. Per the 教学大纲, you are expected to attend 小组课 直播, one per 讲座. A 日历 of 小组课 will appear above once times have been set. Check the bottom of the 日历 above for an indication of what 时间 zone the 日历 is displaying.

小组课 have no attendance limit. We grab via an API your 姓名 and 电子邮件 attached to your Zoom account when you sign into the meeting for attendance so be certain that you can be properly identified, if you want 信用卡 for attending the 小组课! (You are free to switch to a pseudonym, if you wish, during the 小组课 meeting itself!) Watching a recording of a 小组课 does not substitute for the 小组课 attendance requirement in the 课程’s 教学大纲.

小组课 recordings will ordinarily be posted some 时间 on Fridays.

The 课程 treats Zoom just like a classroom. For any meeting that you attend, be sure to:

  • Participate from a quiet space (and not from a bed, car, hammock, plane, or train), where can you listen and speak. If you participate from a 更多 public space, just be sure you won’t be approached or disturbed by others while there.
  • Be sure to participate with your camera turned on, using horizontal (not vertical) 视频, unless your circumstances don’t permit.

If unable to accommodate these norms for some meeting, please 观看 the recording (if any) thereof. And please forgive if the 工作人员 ask you to log out so that all attendees are those fully engaged!
