This 课程 explores the concepts and 算法 at the foundation of modern 人工智能, diving into the ideas that give rise to technologies like game-playing engines, handwriting recognition, and machine translation. Through hands-on 项目, students gain exposure to the theory behind graph 搜索 算法, classification, optimization, 机器学习, large language models, and other topics in 人工智能 as they incorporate them into their own Python programs. By 课程’s end, students emerge with experience in libraries for 机器学习 as well as knowledge of 人工智能 principles that enable them to design intelligent systems of their own.

Prerequisites
CS50x or at least one year of experience with Python.
