How to Prepare

Ultimately, how best to prepare depends on how you learn best. But allow us to recommend that you prioritize your studies per the ordering below.

  1. Review each 周’s 笔记.
  2. Review each 周’s 源代码, if any.
  3. Review each 周’s 幻灯片.
  4. Review each 周’s 短视频, if any.
  5. Attend or 观看 the review sessions.
  6. Take past years’ quizzes and tests and, only after you’ve attempted each of their 问题, review their sample solutions. Realize, though, that some topics covered in past years might not have been covered in this term. Rely on this year’s 讲座 and 问题集 as the official sources for this year’s topics.
  7. Review 问题集’ specifications, sample solutions, and, if any, distribution code.
  8. Review each 讲座’s 视频.
