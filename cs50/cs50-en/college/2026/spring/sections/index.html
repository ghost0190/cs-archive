Sections

Lectures are supplemented by weekly sections led by the course’s teaching fellows. Attendance at sections is expected.

If you have a conflict with your assigned section, please email heads@cs50.harvard.edu ahead of your section time. Unless it is a sickness related conflict, you’d be expected to attend another section that week to make-up your attendance.

Schedule

You need only attend (and register via my.harvard.edu for) one of the below:

  • Tuesdays, 9am-11:45am in Pierce 301 with Annie Sun and Jalil Morris.
  • Tuesdays, 3:45pm-6:30pm in SEC LL2.223 with John Davis and Khang Nguyen.
  • Wednesdays, 6pm-8:45pm in Maxwell Dworkin G125 with David Kong and Thomas Cardenas.

Section slides and recordings will be posted on Wednesdays.
